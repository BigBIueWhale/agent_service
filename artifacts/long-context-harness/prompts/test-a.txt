The workspace holds the complete Keeper of the Lost Cities series as plain text
under `txt/`: eleven files, about 1.55 million words in total.

Read the entire series in full, first page to last, in publication order, and
then write a single continuous summary of the whole series to
`/artifacts/series-summary.md`.

Do all of this yourself. Do not start a subagent for any part of it.

Read every file to its end. Do not sample, skim, spot-check, or infer the
contents of a file you have not read. The series is far larger than your context
window; how you organise the work to cope with that is your decision.

The summary itself should cover, for the series as a whole: the main plot arc
from beginning to end, the principal characters and how they change, and the
major reveals in the order they land.
