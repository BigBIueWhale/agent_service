The workspace holds a single continuous narrative split across 26 plain-text
files under `txt/`, named `chunk_00.txt` through `chunk_25.txt`. Read in that
order they form one unbroken text of roughly 1.3 million characters.

Read every chunk, in order, from `chunk_00.txt` to `chunk_25.txt`, each one to
its end. Then write a single continuous summary of the whole narrative to
`/artifacts/summary.md`.

Do all of this yourself. Do not start a subagent for any part of it.

Do not sample, skim, or infer the contents of a chunk you have not read. The
text is larger than your context window; how you organise the work to cope with
that is your decision.

The summary should cover the main plot arc from beginning to end, the principal
characters and how they change, and the major turning points in the order they
land.
